A complete **Sales Performance Dashboard** built entirely in **Microsoft Excel** – without using Power BI or Tableau.  
This project shows how Excel can still act as a **lightweight BI tool** when structured with the right features.  

---

## 🔑 Project Highlights  

- **📅 Month-wise Sales** → Seasonal trends identified with a **peak in January (40M+)**  
- **🌆 Sales by City** → Karachi (**16.5M+**) leads, followed by Islamabad & Lahore  
- **👥 Sales by Sales Representative** → Top performers: **Steve, Saad, Maria**  
- **📦 Category & Product-wise Analysis** →  
  - **Dell XPS 13** → 20M+  
  - **iPhone 11 Pro** → 17M+  
- **🎛 Slicers & Interactivity** → Easy drill-down by month for deeper insights  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques  

- Microsoft Excel  
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts  
- Slicers for interactivity  
- Conditional Formatting  
- Clean Layout & Design Principles  

---

## 📌 Key Takeaway  

> Excel is not just about rows & columns – when designed properly, it becomes a **powerful visualization and business intelligence tool** for sales tracking, performance monitoring, and decision-making.
